## Question 5: Contact Form Data Retention

**Context**: The spec now includes a contact form (FR-006). It's important to clarify the policy regarding data submitted through this form.

**What we need to know**: How long should data submitted via the contact form be retained, and where?

**Recommended:** Option C (Forward to email immediately, do not retain on server) - For a simple contact form on a showcase website, forwarding to email and not storing sensitive user inquiry data on the server reduces data privacy risks and compliance overhead, especially for an initial build.

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| A      | Retain indefinitely within the CMS/database. |
| B      | Retain for 90 days, then anonymize/delete. |
| C      | Forward to email immediately, do not retain on server. |
