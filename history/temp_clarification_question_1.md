## Question 1: Contact Mechanism for Brand

**Context**: FR-006 states, "The website MUST provide a clear and accessible way for users to contact the brand (e.g., contact form, email address)." The current spec leaves the specific mechanism open.

**What we need to know**: What is the preferred mechanism for users to contact the brand?

**Recommended:** Option B - Embed a basic contact form directly on the website. This provides a more professional and integrated user experience while keeping the scope manageable for an initial build.

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| A      | Simple email link (mailto:) |
| B      | Embed a basic contact form directly on the website. |
| C      | Link to an external contact form/system (e.g., Zendesk, HubSpot). |

You can reply with the option letter (e.g., "A"), accept the recommendation by saying "yes" or "recommended", or provide your own short answer.
